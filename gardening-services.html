Common questions

Everything you need
to know.

Every property is different, but these are the questions our grounds-care conversations often begin with.

How often should commercial grounds be serviced?+

Most commercial sites benefit from weekly or fortnightly visits during active growing periods, with schedules adjusted through cooler months. We recommend a calendar after reviewing your turf, planting, property size and presentation needs.

Can you combine lawn and garden maintenance?+

Yes. Crish Facility Services can coordinate mowing, edging, pruning, garden beds, weeds, seasonal planting, irrigation checks and clean-ups through one clear service program and one point of contact.

Do you work outside business hours?+

We can schedule noisy or access-sensitive work around your operating hours, visitor peaks and site requirements to minimise disruption.

What happens to green waste?+

Green waste generated during scheduled services can be collected and removed as part of your tailored scope, leaving paths, entries and work areas presentation-ready.

Can the program change with the seasons?+

Absolutely. Seasonal adjustment is central to healthy grounds. We refine mowing frequency, irrigation, pruning, planting, feeding and clean-up priorities across the year.
