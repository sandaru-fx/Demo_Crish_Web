02

Subcontractor agreement

Review the complete terms that apply to your engagement with Crish Facility Services Pty. Ltd.

Questions?hr@crishfacilityservices.com.au0435 744 000
Crish Facility Services Pty. Ltd.

Dear Subcontractor,

Thank you for providing your services as a subcontractor with Crish Facility Services Pty. Ltd. We appreciate your collaboration and wish to confirm the terms and conditions that apply to your engagement. Please review this agreement carefully and indicate your acceptance below.

1General+

Personal Documentation

You are required to submit completed forms, a copy of your Working with Children Check, passport, a police check issued within the last six months, and current flu shot or COVID-19 vaccination documentation when requested.

Contact Information

  • Human Resources: hr@crishfacilityservices.com.au
  • Accounts / Invoices: accounts@crishfacilityservices.com.au
  • General inquiries: 0435 744 000

Payment Processing

Payments are processed fortnightly; delays of up to 30 days may occur. Submit your invoice and timesheet by 11:00 AM on or before the end of the fortnight. Late submissions may result in delayed payments.

Invoice Requirements

Invoices must include invoice date and number, facility location, hours worked, ABN and bank-account details.

Confidentiality & Restraint

You agree not to approach or communicate directly with any client of Crish Facility Services Pty. Ltd. without prior written approval.

2Working Agreements+

Attendance and Communication

Notify your supervisor at least four hours before your shift if you cannot attend. Advise in advance if you expect to be late or leave early. Follow work instructions only when issued by authorised CFS personnel and do not discuss work matters with third parties.

Shift Responsibilities

You are responsible for your allocated shifts. Do not send another person without notifying your supervisor. Arrive 5–10 minutes before your scheduled start and remain until the shift ends, even when tasks finish early.

Notice of Resignation

Provide at least two weeks' notice. Failure to do so may result in non-payment for one week.

Personal Protective Equipment

Appropriate PPE, including gloves and face masks, must be worn while performing duties.

Non-Reemployment Clause

After resignation or termination, you agree not to reapply or subcontract with Crish Facility Services Pty. Ltd. or its affiliates for six months.

3Responsibilities and Duties+

Commitment and Reputation

Perform your duties faithfully, diligently and in the best interests of CFS. Maintain and protect the company's professional reputation.

Legal Compliance and Conflict of Interest

Comply with lawful and reasonable directions and relevant laws. Avoid real or perceived conflicts of interest.

Site Security and Cleanliness

  • Secure gates, doors and windows before leaving.
  • Turn off taps, lights and specified appliances.
  • Activate or deactivate alarms as required.
  • Protect occupants, materials, assets, indoor plants, aquariums and all property.

Safety and Reporting

Immediately report safety concerns or incidents to your Site/Area Supervisor, Client Relationship Manager or the CFS after-hours contact.

4Post-Engagement Restrictions+

Non-Disparagement

During and after engagement, you agree not to make defamatory, false or disparaging remarks about CFS, its employees, directors, contractors, clients or operations in written, verbal, electronic or social-media communication.

Client Solicitation

For 24 months after engagement ends, you agree not to directly or indirectly engage, solicit or provide services to a CFS client with whom you had direct or indirect contact, including attempts to divert, take over or accept contracts independently or through another business.

Breach and Penalty

A breach may result in a contractual penalty of up to AUD $25,000, without limiting additional legal remedies including damages and injunctive relief.
